WinMux bundles the complete extension, including its dashboard, logger,
element picker, scriptlets, procedural filters and bundled filter lists.
Local modifications add the stable component key, localize its manifest,
remove Helium's optional DNS permission (WinMux does not expose that API),
gate remote filter requests on WinMux's filter-update consent, and add the
WinMux page-control bridge. The bridge source is distributed with the app.
These modifications to uBlock are licensed under GPL-3.0.
Filter lists and other bundled resources retain their upstream licenses.
